## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the nocturnal resonance and celebratory cadence of Dandiya Raat Nalgonda '26. It marries the mystical depth of a moonlit Deccan night with the kinetic electricity of rhythmic folk percussion and revolving garba circles. The aesthetic bridges traditional festive grandeur with avant-garde editorial festival poster design.

### Design Movements
- **Nocturnal Modernism & Editorial Folk**: A high-impact convergence of structured poster typography, expansive dark-field surfaces, and radiant metallic accents.
- **Glass-Infused Atmosphere**: Frosted royal indigo panels that evoke sheer organza fabrics, twilight haze, and ambient stage lighting.
- **Crisp Editorial Grids**: Precision dividing lines and geometric structural tags inspired by indie festival identity systems, preventing the UI from devolving into generic festive clutter.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Aimed at cultural enthusiasts, youth, festival-goers, performers, and community patrons across Telangana and beyond. The interface instills a sense of anticipation, nocturnal royalty, electric communal energy, and premium exclusivity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive midnight atmosphere punctuated by sharp kinetic accents and incandescent golden highlights.

### Palette Architecture
- **Midnight Foundations (Neutral Base)**:
  - Deepest Cosmos: `#070d1e` (canvas base, modal backing)
  - Midnight Sapphire: `#0a1128` (default background, structural layers)
  - Royal Indigo Void: `#0f1c3f` (elevated cards, container surfaces)
- **Primary (Incandescent Festive Gold)**:
  - Base Gold: `#f6c86a` (primary focal points, festival badges, main CTAs)
  - Amber Flame: `#fbbf24` (hover states, kinetic highlights, active toggles)
  - Gold Pale (Parchment Tint): `#fef3c7` (high-contrast text accents on dark sapphire)
- **Secondary (Royal & Electric Indigo)**:
  - Royal Indigo: `#1d4ed8` / `#2563eb` (structural accents, secondary interactive elements, active borders)
  - Electric Cobalt: `#3b82f6` (focus rings, state highlights, subtle glows)
- **Tertiary (Nocturnal Neon & Stage Teal)**:
  - Cyan Surge: `#38bdf8` (micro-accents, live badge pings, schedule tags)
  - Astral Teal: `#06b6d4` (ambient backlight highlights, tertiary callouts)
- **Parchment & Light Neutrals**:
  - Warm Parchment: `#fbf8f1` (primary typography, headings)
  - Silvered Moon: `#94a3b8` (secondary typography, inactive iconography)
  - Starry Border: `rgba(246, 200, 106, 0.18)` (fine divider lines)

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances raw artistic presence with surgical clarity.

- **Syne (Headlines & Display)**: Chosen for its expressive, dramatic arches and theatrical flare. It anchors festival banners, pass tiers, and marquee titles, evoking the spiraling kinetic rhythm of Dandiya dancers.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Titles)**: Delivers clear, readable body copy across low-light contexts without distracting from the festive ornamentation.
- **Space Grotesk (Labels & Monospaced Metadata)**: Injects an indie festival, ticket-stub aesthetic into dates, venues, timecodes, entry zones, and VIP pass counters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured editorial grid that replicates event playbills and modern cultural festival programs.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12-column responsive fluid grid with `3rem` section margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers cap at `1280px` maximum width.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.

### Rhythm & Alignment
Vertical rhythm adheres to an 8px baseline cadence (`0.5rem` increments). Major editorial divisions leverage asymmetric layout blocks: 7:5 and 8:4 column pairings generate deliberate tension between promotional festival imagery and schedule details.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids generic gray shadows, relying instead on colored cosmic ambient blooms and tonal tiering.

### Tonal Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (Base)**: `#070d1e` solid backdrop with ambient radial gradients of cobalt (`#1d4ed8`) at 12% opacity.
2. **Surface Low**: `#0a1128` with 1px border `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.12)`.
3. **Surface Elevated**: `#0f1c3f` with 1px gold hairline border `rgba(246, 200, 106, 0.18)` and backdrop blur filter of 16px.
4. **Surface Floating (Modals, Dropdowns)**: `#11214d` elevated with a double shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(7, 13, 30, 0.85)` and an ambient aura of `0 0 40px -8px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.28)`.

### Aureate Edge Illumination
Interactive elements in active or selected states carry an inner rim glow: `inset 0 0 12px rgba(246, 200, 106, 0.15)`, simulating stage lights catching gold thread embroidery.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural edge (`roundedness: 1`).

- **Standard Base Radii**: `0.25rem` (4px) across inputs, buttons, and badges to preserve the crisp festival-ticket and print-poster character.
- **Surface & Card Radii (`rounded-lg`)**: `0.5rem` (8px) for festival passes, artist cards, and schedule segments.
- **Overlay & Modal Radii (`rounded-xl`)**: `0.75rem` (12px) for booking dialogs and media viewports.
- **Angle Insets & Ticket Notches**: Select promotional badges and ticket containers may implement chamfered or notched corner cuts (4px to 8px) to evoke traditional passes and cultural geometry without sacrificing modern elegance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Festive Gold)**: Background `#f6c86a`, text `#070d1e`, font `Space Grotesk` uppercase semi-bold. Subtle glow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(246, 200, 106, 0.45)`). 4px border-radius.
- **Secondary (Indigo Stage)**: Background `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.16)`, border `1px solid #3b82f6`, text `#fbf8f1`. On hover, fills to `#2563eb`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent with text `#f6c86a`, trailing directional glyph, and an underline that expands on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Live / Event Status**: Background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`, text `#38bdf8`, border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)`. Space Grotesk tracking `0.06em`.
- **Pass Category**: Background `rgba(246, 200, 106, 0.12)`, text `#fbbf24`, border `1px solid rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.25)`.

### Cards (Passes & Artist Spotlights)
- Composed of `#0f1c3f` background with an inset gold hairline border (`rgba(246, 200, 106, 0.2)`).
- Headers display `Syne` titling over subtle grain overlays.
- Pass cards feature a dashed divider line simulating tear-off admission vouchers.

### Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Form Inputs**: Fill `#0a1128`, border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.25)`, text `#fbf8f1`. On focus, border changes to `#f6c86a` with an electric cobalt ambient shadow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.25)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Square 4px and circle containers filled with `#070d1e`; when checked, dynamic fill `#f6c86a` with `#070d1e` inner mark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Garba Circle Slot Picker**: Concentric circular track UI allowing attendees to book specific dance arena time-windows (Ras Kendra vs. Prangan Ring).
- **Dandiya Pair Voucher Badge**: Digital ticket pass counter with metallic holographic gold strip detailing and dynamic event entry QR framing.
- **Rhythm Track Schedule Bar**: Horizontal kinetic timeline showcasing Dhol performers, DJ sets, and Aarti ceremonies with cyan active progress indicators.
